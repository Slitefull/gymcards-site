Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026 · Last updated 17 September 2026

Gym Cards is a workout diary that keeps everything you record on your phone. We do not run a server for your data and there is no Gym Cards account.

The short version. We collect nothing, and we cannot see your workouts, photos or body measurements. They leave your device only when you export or share them yourself, or when you turn on backup to your own iCloud or Google Drive.

Who we are

Gym Cards is published by Maksym Tytechko. You can reach us at 0xmaksym@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to a private database and a private folder inside the app's own storage area, which only Gym Cards can read:

CategoryWhat it is
WorkoutsDates, times, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, warm-up flags, personal-record flags, free-text notes, favourites
Exercise libraryExercise names, hints and descriptions that you type
Photos and videosProgress photos and their alignment, technique videos, month cover images and your profile picture, copied into the app's private folder when you attach them
Body measurementsBody weight, body-fat percentage and circumference measurements you choose to record, with their dates
Goals and achievementsThe goals you set, when they were reached, and the achievements you unlocked
ProfileThe display name, motto and avatar you set inside the app
SettingsTheme, app icon, language, units, week start, text size, haptics, reminder schedule, backup preferences, and whether Gym Cards Pro is active

What we collect

Nothing. Gym Cards contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking identifiers and no third-party SDKs that report usage. The app uses the network only for the purchases, updates and optional backup described below, and none of them sends your diary to us.

Because we collect no personal data, there is no data for us to sell, share, or disclose to anyone, and no profiling or automated decision-making takes place.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

PermissionWhy
Photo libraryOnly so you can pick a photo or video to attach to a workout, an exercise, a month cover or your profile. The app reads the item you pick and copies it into its own private storage. It never browses or uploads your library.
NotificationsOnly to deliver the local workout reminders you schedule yourself, on the days and at the time you choose. Reminders are generated on your device. There is no push server.
VibrationHaptic feedback on taps and saves. You can turn it off in Settings.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar or advertising-identifier access. It asks for permission to write to Apple Health only when you turn that on yourself in Settings.

When data leaves your device

  • Export CSV, JSON backup or month PDF. The file is written to a temporary folder and handed to your operating system's share sheet. You choose the destination: email, cloud drive, messenger, or nowhere. We never see it.
  • Sharing a photo, a recap or a card through the same system share sheet.
  • Automatic backup (Gym Cards Pro, off until you turn it on). On iPhone the app copies your diary as a JSON file, and your photos and videos if you allow it, into the private Gym Cards folder of your own iCloud account. On Android it copies them into the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive after you sign in with Google. Google Sign-In is used only for this, and it asks only for access to that hidden app folder. Apple or Google store these files under their own privacy policies. We never receive them.
  • Purchases. Gym Cards Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. They process the payment and tell the app whether Pro is owned. We never see your payment details.
  • App updates. When the app starts it may ask Expo's update service whether newer app code is available and download it. The request carries the app version and platform, not your data.

Once you hand a file to another app or service, that app's or service's own privacy policy governs what happens to it.

Home screen widgets

To draw the widgets, the app writes a small summary (workout counts, training days and colours) to storage that only the app and its own widgets on your device can read. It never leaves the device.

Device backups

Gym Cards opts out of Android's automatic device backup, so your workout database and media are not copied there. On iOS, whether app data is included in an iCloud or encrypted local device backup is controlled by your own device settings, which are managed by Apple, not by us. To move your data deliberately, use the automatic backup above or the JSON export and import in Settings.

Children

Gym Cards is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all.

Your control over your data

  • See it: everything is visible in the app.
  • Export it: Settings, Data, Export CSV or JSON.
  • Delete a workout: it goes to Trash, and you can restore it or purge it permanently.
  • Delete a cloud backup: on iPhone, Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Gym Cards, Delete Data. On Android, Google Drive on the web, Settings, Manage apps, Gym Cards, Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes the database, the media folder and all settings from your device.

Since we hold no personal data, requests to access, correct, port or erase data held by us have nothing to act on: you already hold and control all of it. If you have a question about that, write to us anyway and we will answer.

Health data

Body weight, body-fat percentage and body measurements are health-related information. They are stored on your device and, only if you turn on backup, in your own iCloud or Google Drive. We never read them. If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, the app writes your workouts there: the workout type, its start time and its end time. It reads nothing from Apple Health, and nothing from Apple Health ends up in a backup. You can turn it off in Settings or in the Health app at any time. Gym Cards does not connect to Google Fit or Health Connect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
